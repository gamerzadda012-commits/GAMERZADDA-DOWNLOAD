Contact GAMERZADDA

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Need help with your account, a tournament, a payment or a withdrawal request? Contact the GAMERZADDA support team using the details below.

Email Support

Email: gamerzaddahelp@gmail.com. Include your registered email or user ID, the issue you are experiencing and any relevant tournament or transaction reference.

Tournament and Match Help

For tournament questions, include the tournament name or ID, match date and a clear description of the issue. If requested by the tournament rules, keep the relevant screen recording or proof available.

Payment and Withdrawal Help

For payment or withdrawal concerns, include the transaction or withdrawal request reference, amount and date. Never send your password, UPI PIN, card PIN or OTP.

Account and Privacy Requests

For account or privacy requests, contact us from your registered email where possible. We may need to verify account ownership before making changes or sharing account-specific information.

Support Hours and Response

Response times may vary depending on request volume and the information needed to review your issue. Keep your request details available if follow-up is required.
