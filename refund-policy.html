Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Check before payment

Review the tournament entry fee, rules, eligibility and prize details before confirming a payment or entry.

2. Refund eligibility

Refund eligibility depends on the specific transaction, tournament status, displayed terms, applicable law and payment provider rules. A refund is not guaranteed for every completed entry or transaction.

3. Failed or duplicate payments

If a payment appears debited but the app does not show the expected result, or if you believe a duplicate charge occurred, contact support with the transaction reference, date, amount and relevant screenshots. Never send your password, OTP or full payment credentials.

4. Processing

After review, approved refunds are handled through the applicable payment method or provider process. Processing times may vary.

5. Contact

Email gamerzaddahelp@gmail.com with the subject “Payment / Refund Query”. Include only the information needed to identify the transaction.

This general policy must be checked against the actual payment, tournament and legal requirements before publication.
