Refund Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains how GAMERZADDA reviews payment, tournament-entry and withdrawal-related concerns. Outcomes depend on the transaction status, tournament rules and applicable law.

Tournament Entry Fees

Before joining a tournament, check its entry fee, format, schedule and rules. Entry-fee refunds are not automatic. If a tournament is cancelled, materially changed, or a duplicate or incorrect charge occurs, contact support so the case can be reviewed under the applicable rules.

Incorrect or Duplicate Payments

If you believe you were charged incorrectly or more than once, email gamerzaddahelp@gmail.com with your registered account details, transaction reference, amount, date and a short description. Do not send passwords or OTPs.

Failed Transactions

A payment may show as pending or failed while the payment service provider updates its status. We will review the available transaction information. Any reversal or refund timing may depend on the provider and banking network.

Withdrawals

A withdrawal request is not the same as a refund. Eligible withdrawals are processed according to the app's withdrawal rules, verification checks, minimum limits, charges and payment-provider processing. If a withdrawal is delayed or rejected, contact support with the request details.

Promotional Rewards

Signup bonuses, referral rewards, spin rewards, scratch cards, vouchers and other promotional benefits are subject to the offer terms. They are not cash refunds and may not be transferable or withdrawable unless the offer explicitly permits it.

Non-Refundable Situations

Except where required by applicable law or stated in the relevant tournament or offer rules, fees may not be refundable for a user's failure to participate, disqualification for rule violations, incorrect game details supplied by the user, or a change of mind after joining.

How to Request a Review

Contact gamerzaddahelp@gmail.com and include your registered email or user ID, transaction/request reference, amount, date and relevant screenshots. We may ask for additional information to verify the request.

Contact

Support email: gamerzaddahelp@gmail.com.
